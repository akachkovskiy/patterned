Privacy Policy

Patterned: Your Privacy is Our Science.

1. 100% Offline-First Architecture

Patterned is designed as a standalone offline application. Your database is stored locally on your device's secure internal storage. No cloud servers are used for data storage, synchronization, or processing.

2. Zero Data Collection

As the developers, we have no access to your personal logs, metrics, or research results. We do not use analytics tools to track your specific entries, identify your patterns, or monitor your individual performance.

3. No Identity Tracking

You are never required to create an account, provide an email address, or share any personally identifiable information (PII). Your data belongs to you, and your identity remains protected by design.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Your data persists only in your local SQLite database. If you delete the application or clear the system cache, all data is permanently removed.

In compliance with Play Market regulations, we provide a dedicated "Delete Data & Account" screen within the app settings to allow you to manually wipe your entire local database at any time.

5. Third-Party Services

The only external communication occurs through RevenueCat (to handle Pro feature activations) and the official Google Play Store. These services handle transactional metadata only and never have access to your personal logging data or experiment results.

⚠️ Statistical Tool Only

Patterned is strictly a mathematical and statistical analysis tool. It is not designed for medical or health diagnostic purposes. The app does not provide health advice, medical diagnoses, symptom tracking, or clinical treatment recommendations. All insights are purely based on statistical correlation coefficients and should be interpreted as mathematical observations only.
