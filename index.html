Professional Privacy Policy

Patterned: Your Data. Your Sovereignty.

1. Privacy-by-Design

Patterned (the "Application") is architected as an offline-first development. We operate under a zero-access model. We do not possess any technical means to view, collect, or store your personal data on external servers.

2. Data Governance & Sovereignty

All data, including health-related logs, lifestyle entries, and experimental metrics, is stored exclusively within your device's secure local storage. The Application does not transmit your personal research logs to any external cloud infrastructure.

3. Anonymity & Zero Collection

We do not require account registration, email collection, or identity verification. As the developers, we have zero access to the data points you record or the statistical results generated by our algorithms.

4. Regulatory Compliance (GDPR/CCPA)

While we do not store your data, our practices are built to align with the principles of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). You maintain the absolute right to access and erase your data at any time via the internal tools provided in the Application.

5. Third-Party Frameworks

Transactional metadata is processed via RevenueCat (for subscription management) and the Google Play Store. These providers are strictly prohibited from accessing your internal logging database and handle only the telemetry required for official app store operations.
