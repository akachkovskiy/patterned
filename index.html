Privacy Policy

Patterned

1. Offline-First Approach

Patterned is an offline-first application. All data, including your logs, metrics, and experimental results, is stored exclusively on your device's local storage. We do not use cloud servers for data storage.

2. Zero Data Collection

We do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data. We do not require account registration or email addresses. As developers, we have no access to your data or activity.

3. Complete Anonymity

Your identity and data remain completely anonymous. We do not use third-party analytics or tracking frameworks to monitor your specific entries or usage patterns.

4. Data Deletion

You maintain full control over your data. You can delete your entire local database at any time using the "Delete Data & Account" feature in the app settings, which will permanently wipe all information.

5. Third-Party Services

The app uses RevenueCat strictly for processing in-app purchases and communicates with the Google Play Store. These services handle transactional metadata only and cannot access your local logging data.
